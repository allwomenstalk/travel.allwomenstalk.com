# 9 Must\-Visit Places in Helsinki That Are Simply Amazing

If you’re planning a trip to Finland this autumn, you might be wondering about the top places to visit in Helsinki to make the most of your travel itinerary. You’ll be pleased to know that autumn in Helsinki offers mild weather with average temperatures around 14–16°C, perfect for sightseeing. That doesn’t mean you shouldn’t pack a cozy sweater or light jacket though, and be sure to bring comfortable walking shoes. With so many interesting places in Helsinki to explore, you could easily find yourself on your feet all day! Whether you're looking for vibrant design hubs, serene nature, or historic architecture, here are the must-see places in Helsinki you simply can’t afford to miss during your Nordic adventure.

## 1. The Design District

![The Design District](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/09/13172944_5716_600x400.jpg)

Whether you’re passionate about interior design, adore architecture, or love creative fashion, visiting Helsinki’s Design District is essential. Spanning across Punavuori, Kaartinkaupunki, Kamppi, and Ullanlinna, this vibrant creative hub features over 200 boutiques, galleries, design studios, and cafes. The best way to explore the area is on foot along tailored walking routes. You can find detailed maps and shop listings on the official [Design District Helsinki](https://designdistrict.fi/en/) hub. If you care about sustainable fashion and eco-friendly home goods, the sustainable shopping route will be right up your street! After shopping, consider unwinding at a [traditional Finnish sauna](#6) later in your journey.

Key highlights in the Design District include:

- Local artisan jewelry and modern interior boutiques
- Independent contemporary art galleries
- Cozy cafes serving authentic Finnish pastries

## 2. Linnanmaki Amusement Park

![Linnanmaki Amusement Park](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/09/13173038_2231_590x350.jpg)

Opened in 1950, [Linnanmäki Amusement Park](https://www.linnanmaki.fi/en/) is one of the most beloved family places to see in Helsinki. The park features a thrilling blend of modern rollercoasters alongside nostalgic rides perfect for younger travelers. Don’t miss a ride on Vuoristorata, the classic wooden rollercoaster built in 1951—it remains a legendary attraction operated by skilled wooden-brake pilots! Once you’ve had your fill of adrenaline, enjoy the classic amusement arcades, take in live performances at the outdoor stage, or savor panoramic city views from the panorama tower.

## 3. Seurasaari Open-Air Museum

![Seurasaari Open-Air Museum](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/09/13173119_2650_600x338.jpg)

While some travelers seek out beaches or shopping, others prefer immersing themselves in rich heritage and culture. If you love history, the [Seurasaari Open-Air Museum](https://www.kansallismuseo.fi/en/seurasaarenulkomuseo) is one of the finest sites to see in Helsinki. Located on a tranquil island connected to the mainland by a wooden bridge, the museum exhibits historic wooden buildings relocated from all across Finland, dating back to the 18th and 19th centuries. Walking through Seurasaari offers an authentic glimpse into rural Finnish life across different social classes. Take a guided tour or watch craft demonstrations led by guides in traditional national costumes for a truly memorable cultural experience.

## 4. Temppeliaukio Church

![Temppeliaukio Church](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/09/13173143_0700_600x401.jpg)

Opened in 1969 and designed by architects Timo and Tuomo Suomalainen, [Temppeliaukio Church](https://temppeliaukio.fi/en/) (famously known as the Rock Church) is undoubtedly one of the helsinki best places to visit. Directly excavated into solid granite bedrock, the interior is bathed in natural light streaming through a magnificent copper dome skylight. Due to its exceptional natural acoustics, the church regularly serves as a premier venue for acoustic music and evening concerts. Make sure to bring your camera to capture the breathtaking rock walls and ambient lighting, or take a quick break here before heading out to explore [local art galleries](#7) across the city.

## 5. Helsinki Zoo

![Helsinki Zoo](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/09/13173353_0106_600x413.jpg)

You might think that once you've visited one zoo you've seen them all, but Korkeasaari Zoo (Helsinki Zoo) is truly unique. Founded in 1889, it stands as one of the world’s oldest zoos, situated entirely on its own island in the Baltic archipelago. Home to more than 150 animal species and hundreds of plant species, the zoo plays a vital role in global biodiversity and endangered species conservation programs. Accessible by bridge or seasonal ferry, it is a fantastic destination for a family day out or a scenic romantic stroll along island trails.

## 6. Kaurila Sauna

![Kaurila Sauna](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/09/13173440_5867_600x403.jpg)

Helsinki is a fantastic destination to relax and unwind, and experiencing an authentic sauna is an essential part of Finnish culture. Kaurila Sauna offers a historic wood-heated log sauna experience that takes you back to 19th-century traditions. The gentle heat and soothing steam help ease traveling fatigue and leave you thoroughly refreshed. If you're traveling with friends or family, booking a private session allows you to relax in cozy wooden lounge rooms and enjoy refreshments together after a steam. It's a wonderful contrast after spending a lively day at [family amusement parks](#2) or walking through city centers.

## 7. Unknown Cargo Gallery

![Unknown Cargo Gallery](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/09/13173912_5096_600x400.jpg)

If you enjoy innovative art experiences, Helsinki's vibrant contemporary art scene features numerous underground spaces and concept galleries like the former Unknown Cargo Gallery. The concept behind blind exhibitions allows art lovers to appreciate visual creations without being influenced by artist reputation or background—revealing creator identities only after the show concludes. From emerging Nordic talents to international creators, exploring Helsinki's independent art spots offers a refreshing, thought-provoking addition to your sightseeing route.

## 8. The Helsinki Baltic Herring Fair

![The Helsinki Baltic Herring Fair](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/09/13173937_4157_600x400.jpg)

The name might sound surprising at first, but the Helsinki Baltic Herring Fair (Silakkamarkkinat) is one of the oldest and most atmospheric traditional events in Finland, dating all the way back to 1743. Held annually every October at Market Square (Kauppatori), fishing boats dock at the harbor to sell freshly caught and marinated herring, traditional archipelago bread, and seasonal island specialties. Even if you're just browsing, the vibrant waterfront atmosphere, steaming food stalls, and coastal maritime views make it one of the most memorable places to visit in Helsinki during autumn.

What to try at the Baltic Herring Fair:

- Traditional marinated herring in mustard or rowanberry sauce
- Warm archipelago rye bread with Finnish butter
- Freshly grilled salmon soup by the harbor

## 9. Stadium Tower

![Stadium Tower](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/09/13174030_1012_600x450.jpg)

Originally constructed in 1938 for the Olympic Games, the iconic Helsinki Olympic Stadium features a striking 72-meter high tower that provides unmatched panoramic views across the city skyline and surrounding sea. After taking in the sights from the top, you can explore the Sports Museum of Finland (TAHTO) located right at the stadium complex to learn about the nation's rich athletic history. Visiting the tower is an absolute highlight for photography enthusiasts, so make sure your camera battery is fully charged before taking the lift up!

Whatever your idea of a perfect getaway, Helsinki delivers a delightful mix of modern design, nature, and rich cultural heritage. These 9 must see places in Helsinki are just the beginning of what Finland has to offer. If you have extra time, consider taking scenic day trips to nearby historic towns like Porvoo or exploring the natural trails of Nuuksio National Park.
