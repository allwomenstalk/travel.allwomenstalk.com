# 9 Famous Places to Visit in Ecuador: Top Tourist Attractions & Points of Interest

While most travelers head to Brazil, Peru, and Chile when traveling to South America, they often overlook some of the most stunning attractions in Ecuador. This vibrant country offers an incredible variety of landscapes and cultures packed into an accessible region, making it one of the top places to visit in Ecuador for adventurous travelers. If you are seeking authentic South American travel experiences, exploring famous places in Ecuador will leave you inspired and wanting to discover more.

Whether you are planning a short getaway or an extensive South American tour, here are some highlights of what makes Ecuador tourist attractions so special:

- Active snow-capped volcanoes and high-altitude mountain hiking

- Amazon rainforest expeditions and whitewater river rafting

- Rich colonial history and authentic indigenous craft markets

- Unrivaled biodiversity and wildlife encounters in island ecosystems

To help you plan your itinerary, here is a quick overview of key highlights across the country:

| Attraction | Location | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cotopaxi | Cotopaxi National Park | High-altitude hiking & volcanic views |
| Tena | Napo Province | Amazon rainforest & whitewater rafting |
| Baños | Tungurahua Province | Adventure sports & thermal hot springs |
| Galapagos Islands | Pacific Ocean | Unique wildlife & eco-tours |

## 1. Cotopaxi

![Cotopaxi Volcano in Ecuador](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2013/08/1_cotopaxi_600x450.jpg)

Get closer to nature with a trip to [Cotopaxi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotopaxi), one of the most breathtaking tourist attractions in Ecuador! Cotopaxi is a majestic snow-capped stratovolcano that forms part of the Pacific Ring of Fire, rising to an impressive 5,897 meters (19,347 feet). As the second-highest peak in the country and one of the highest active volcanoes in the world, it has recorded dozens of eruptions since the 1700s. Situated within Cotopaxi National Park just 28 km (17 mi) south of Quito, it stands as one of the most beautiful places in Ecuador. Convenient day trips are readily available from the capital, offering guided hikes and panoramic mountain views.

## 2. Tena

Tena is widely celebrated as the quintessential South American jungle gateway, serving as the primary launch pad for rainforest adventure excursions. Situated deep in the Amazon basin of Ecuador at the meeting point of the Tena and Pano rivers, it is one of the most interesting places to visit in Ecuador for outdoor enthusiasts. Whitewater rafting and kayaking along the surrounding river systems are regarded among the best in the world. Additionally, Tena offers visitors a wide array of local eateries, eco-lodges, and professional guide services. For travelers seeking a contrast to high-altitude spots like [Cotopaxi](#1), traveling roughly 185 km (114 mi) southwest of Quito brings you right into the heart of the Amazon, accessible via daily bus routes and private shuttles.

## 3. Catedral Nueva, Cuenca

![Catedral Nueva in Cuenca Ecuador](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2013/08/3_catedral-nueva-cuenca_600x400.jpg)

Witness a magnificent architectural marvel at the [Catedral Nueva in Cuenca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuenca,_Ecuador). Construction on this iconic cathedral began in 1880 and spanned nearly a century to complete. The structure features elegant pink Italian marble floors and three distinctive sky-blue tiled domes imported from Czechoslovakia. Because historic downtown Cuenca is recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage site, visitors can explore exceptionally preserved colonial plazas, cobblestone streets, and historic landmarks. As Ecuador's third-largest city, Cuenca remains one of the premier Ecuador points of interest and a must-see destination for culture and history lovers.

## 4. Nariz Del Diablo

![Nariz Del Diablo Devil's Nose Train Ecuador](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2013/08/4_nariz-del-diablo_600x398.jpg)

Experience one of South America's most thrilling railway journeys on the Devil’s Nose (Nariz del Diablo). This dramatic cliffside train route descends steep mountain switchbacks cut directly into vertical rock faces. Built in the late 1800s, this historic engineering feat earned its dramatic name during its perilous construction. The excursion routes through the breathtaking Andes between Alausí and Sibambe stations, making it one of the most famous tourist attractions in Ecuador for railway enthusiasts and photographers alike. Sitting on the right side of the carriage yields the most spectacular scenery during the descent.

## 5. Otavalo Market

![Otavalo Market Artisan Crafts Ecuador](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2013/08/5_otavalo-market_600x428.jpg)

Discover an artisan shopper’s dream at Otavalo Market, located roughly 90 km (60 mi) northwest of Quito in the Andean highlands. The indigenous Otavaleño artisans are internationally renowned for their masterfully woven textiles, handcrafted garments, intricate jewelry, and wood carvings. While vendors sell crafts throughout the week, the bustling Saturday market expands across Plaza de Ponchos into one of the largest open-air markets in South America. It stands as one of the most popular places to see in Ecuador to experience rich Andean traditions and pick up handcrafted souvenirs.

## 6. Baños

![Baños Thermal Hot Springs Ecuador](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2013/08/6_ba-os_600x401.jpg)

Nestled in the lush foothills beneath the active Tungurahua Volcano, Baños (Baños de Agua Santa) is the undisputed adventure capital of the country and one of the top ecuador tourist spots. Named after its naturally heated mineral hot springs, this vibrant town draws thrill-seekers from around the globe. Similar to the river expeditions in [Tena](#2), Baños provides access to whitewater rafting, mountain biking, canyoning, zip-lining, and waterfall tours—with over 60 spectacular waterfalls located in the immediate area. It ranks among the most beautiful places in Ecuador for combining relaxation with adrenaline-pumping outdoor sports.

## 7. Montañita

World-class ocean waves and a relaxed beach vibe make Montañita one of the most sought-after attractions in Ecuador. Once a quiet fishing village on the Santa Elena peninsula, Montañita has evolved into South America's premier surf hub, famous for consistent right-point breaks driven by both northern and southern swells. Located about 170 km (106 mi) west of Guayaquil, it features budget-friendly accommodations, seaside cafes, and lively evening entertainment, making it one of the most fun tourist places in Ecuador for beach lovers and coastal travelers.

## 8. San Francisco Church, Quito

![San Francisco Church Historic Center Quito](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2013/08/8_san-francisco-church-quito_600x393.jpg)

Anchoring the historic center of Quito, the Iglesia y Monasterio de San Francisco represents the largest historic architectural complex in Latin America. Built over the foundations of an ancient Incan palace, this imposing two-block stone monastery houses an extraordinary museum containing more than 3,500 colonial artworks, sculptures, and religious paintings. Along with religious sites like [Catedral Nueva in Cuenca](#3), San Francisco Church showcases Ecuador's rich artistic legacy, notably featuring the famed carved sculpture 'Winged Virgin of Quito' by Bernardo de Legarda. It stands out as one of the essential ecuador main attractions for city explorers.

## 9. Galapagos Islands

![Galapagos Islands Wildlife Ecuador](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2013/08/9_galapagos-islands_580x386.jpg)

Experience the wonder of evolution firsthand with a visit to the extraordinary [Galapagos Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gal%C3%A1pagos_Islands). Serving as the primary inspiration for Charles Darwin’s theory of natural selection, this volcanic archipelago situated 1,000 km (620 mi) off the Pacific coast is undoubtedly the crown jewel of tourism and attractions in Ecuador. Visitors can observe endemic species in their natural habitats, including marine iguanas, giant Galapagos tortoises, playful sea lions, and blue-footed boobies. Five of the major islands host sustainable eco-lodges and local communities. For official visitor guidance and travel destination details, check out [Ecuador Travel](https://www.ecuador.travel/) before booking certified guide excursions or island cruises.
