# 8 Fun and Memorable Things to Do in Las Vegas Besides Gambling

Have you ever thought about visiting Nevada but wondered about the best things to do in las vegas besides gamble? I felt the exact same way back in the day! I had no idea why my friends were so excited to haul butt to a desert to sit in a casino, but I was completely wrong. If you are searching for stuff to do in vegas besides gamble, the famous 4.2-mile Strip offers an endless array of world-class entertainment, fine dining, and unique attractions. Today's Sin City is a far cry from the past when it was all about poker tables and cigars. Whether you are looking for cool things to do in vegas besides gamble or unique things to do in las vegas besides gamble with friends, allow me to show you a sampling of the many amazing offerings Las Vegas has to offer!

## 1. Wine and Dine

You gotta eat, and one of the best things to do in las vegas that s not the casino is exploring the incredible culinary scene gathered right on the Strip! You can try international cuisine prepared by world-renowned celebrity chefs, feast at some of the largest buffets you will ever lay your eyes on, and enjoy sweet treats galore. If you have a sweet tooth like me, be sure to stop by the [Sugar Factory](https://www.sugarfactory.com/) for an over-the-top milkshake, indulge in artisanal treats at local bakeries, or enjoy gourmet dining that rivals any culinary capital in the world. Afterwards, you can unwind or plan your next step to [see a show](#2) for an unforgettable evening.

## 2. See a Show

Even if you’re not a big theater buff, catching a live performance is one of the most fun things to do in las vegas other than gamble. The city is the entertainment capital of the world, featuring productions that cater to pretty much every taste, including stand-up comedy, mind-bending magic, world-class dance, and hit Broadway musicals. Spectacular resident acts like [Cirque du Soleil](https://www.cirquedusoleil.com/) offer breathtaking acrobatics that will leave you spellbound. Since top-tier performances can be on the pricier side, it helps to do some research before heading out to narrow down your options and reserve your seats early.

## 3. Shop

Shopping is another top choice when looking for what to do in vegas besides gamble, and in my opinion, it is easily one of the best! The Las Vegas Strip gives you access to some of the most illustrious fashion designers, luxury boutiques, and unique shops from around the globe, many of which are conveniently located under a single roof. If you don’t have the cash to drop at high-end houses like Dior or Hermès, window shopping is just as entertaining, and you can always drop some dough at fun, budget-friendly spots like the iconic M&M's World!

- The Forum Shops at Caesars Palace
- Grand Canal Shoppes at The Venetian
- Miracle Mile Shops at Planet Hollywood

## 4. Shark Reef Aquarium

Seeing an ocean aquarium in the middle of a desert might not be the first thing on your itinerary, but it is definitely one of the most extraordinary things to do in las vegas besides gambling! Head over to the [Mandalay Bay Resort](https://mandalaybay.mgmresorts.com/) and check out their stunning Shark Reef Aquarium, where you can admire sea turtles, giant rays, jellyfish, and over 2,000 marine animals for less than the cost of a round of drinks at a Strip bar! If you are feeling adventurous, certified divers can even book an underwater dive with the sharks. Afterwards, you can relax by indulging in one of the city's [luxurious spas](#8).

## 5. Dance

Las Vegas nightlife and mega-clubs are just as famous as the casinos surrounding them, making clubbing a popular pick for what is there to do in las vegas besides gambling. Even if you say you hate to dance, you can still have a blast taking in the impressive light displays, chatting with fellow travelers from across the world, or sipping handcrafted cocktails. The nightlife scene features internationally famous DJs and stunning rooftop venues. Even if you don't feel like shaking that moneymaker, experiencing a Vegas venue at least once is a must-do—and who knows, you might even spot a celebrity in the VIP section!

## 6. Thrill Rides

The Las Vegas Strip actually boasts incredible theme-park rides despite its urban setting, so be sure to add adrenaline adventures to your list of fun things to do in vegas besides gamble! Destinations like New York-New York, the STRAT Observation Tower, Circus Circus Adventuredome, and Fremont Street offer thrilling ways to get your heart pumping without touching a slot machine or ordering alcohol. From indoor roller coasters to controlled free-fall sky jumps, Sin City has you covered when it comes to high-octane thrills!

- The Big Apple Coaster at New York-New York
- Insanity and X-Scream at The STRAT Tower
- SlotZilla Zipline at Fremont Street Experience

## 7. Not-so-Thrill Rides

If the idea of a roller coaster makes your stomach turn, don't worry—there are plenty of peaceful things to do around vegas besides gambling. [The Venetian Resort](https://www.venetianlasvegas.com/) offers gentle gondola rides along outdoor and indoor canals that are serene, charming, and deeply romantic! If you are open to venturing slightly off the Strip, you can enjoy scenic horse trail rides, Grand Canyon helicopter tours, or desert dune buggy excursions that the whole family will love.

| Gondola Rides | The Venetian | Romantic Canal Cruises |

## 8. Spas

Las Vegas resort spas are extraordinarily luxurious and decadent, making a spa day one of the most rewarding things to do in las vegas that s not the casino. Everything from the five-star service to the peaceful marble decor is absolutely top-notch. Even though high-end treatments can be on the pricier side, you truly deserve a pampering break on vacation! If you are traveling on a budget, compare spa day passes and guest reviews to splurge on a single signature massage or facial while cutting back on other expenses.

If you were on the fence about visiting Nevada, I hope this guide to things to do in las vegas besides gambling has convinced you to give it a go! There is so much to see, taste, and experience along the famous 4.2-mile Strip and beyond that you are bound to find activities you absolutely love. What is your favorite thing to do in Vegas when taking a break from the casino floor?
