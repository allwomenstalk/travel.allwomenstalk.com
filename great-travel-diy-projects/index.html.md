# 10 Genius Travel DIY Projects and Crafts to Keep You Organized on the Go

Travel DIY Projects range from organizers to clever crafts that keep your travel mate occupied and keep you sane on long journeys. Before you head out on your next adventure, explore these **travel craft projects**. You might just find something from this list of **travel DIY ideas** that will save you money, keep your luggage organized, and make your trip run smoother than ever.

| Travel DIY Project | Skill Level | Key Materials |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tech Organizer | Beginner | Old book cover, elastic bands |
| Portable Art Kit | Easy | Mini watercolor set, tin box |
| Leather Passport Holder | Intermediate | Leather scrap, waxed thread |

## 1. Tech Organizer

_ Wires and cables frequently get tangled in travel bags during long trips. Finding a phone charger at the bottom of a crowded weekender can be frustrating. Creating a custom pouch or book-bound holder for [DIY tech projects](https://diy.allwomenstalk.com/) keeps everything neat and accessible. Book lovers in particular will enjoy transforming an old hardcover into a discreet electronics case.

- Keeps charging cords tangle-free
- Repurposes a vintage book frame
- Slips easily into any carry-on bag

## 2. Art Kit

![Art Kit](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2012/03/2-1024x680_1024x680.jpg)

This **travel DIY idea** is perfect for artists and creative travelers who love sketching on the go. Lugging a bulky paint set takes up precious luggage space. Creating a compact watercolor kit using an Altoids tin or small palette makes painting while traveling effortless and light. Explore wonderful [travel crafts](https://travel.allwomenstalk.com/) for your next getaway.

[Tutorial](http://onegoldenapple.blogspot.com/2011/04/watercolors-to-go.html)

## 3. Pocket + Hanger

![Pocket + Hanger](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2012/03/31_500x375.jpg)

Packing formal wear or a wedding outfit requires extra care to prevent wrinkles. Hotel hangers often lack storage for accessories like belts, jewelry, or ties. Crafting a garment hanger with a sewn fabric pocket offers an easy solution for keeping your outfit and accessories together in your hotel closet.

[Tutorial](https://www.instructables.com/Make-a-travel-pocket/)

## 4. Sewing Kit

![Sewing Kit](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2012/03/41_500x300.jpg)

ADORABLE! A cat-themed felt mending kit stores needles, thread, buttons, and small scissors securely. It keeps essential sewing supplies organized in a lightweight, compact case. The way the scissors are stored is clever and convenient for travel.

- Compact and lightweight design
- Safely secures scissors and needles
- Handy for quick wardrobe repairs

[Tutorial](https://sew4home.com/felt-sewing-kit-for-travel-mending-a-hand-stitching-project/)

## 5. Drawing Kit

[img.allw.mn](https://img.allw.mn/content/travel/2012/03/5_1024x683.png)
While portable watercolors suit adults, a fabric drawing tote keeps kids entertained on road trips. Packing crayons, colored pencils, and a notepad into a portable pouch prevents boredom during long car rides. If you don't want the kids to ask "Are we there yet?" every 5 minutes, making this drawing kit is a must. If you want more creative travel ideas, check out the [art kit](#2) above for adults.

[Tutorial](https://cookcleancraft.com/2011/09/travel-drawing-kit/)

## 6. Travel Tote

![Travel Tote](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2012/03/61_360x301.jpg)

If you don't want the first gadget organizer we featured here, this Martha Stewart idea might be more up your alley. A tea towel can be easily converted into a simple gadget pouch or organizer tote. This easy upcycling project requires minimal sewing skills and repurposes household textiles. If you prefer a hard-cover case over fabric, look back at the [tech organizer](#1) project.

[Tutorial](https://web.archive.org/web/http://www.marthastewart.com/266189/gadget-travel-tote)

## 7. Changing Pad

![Changing Pad](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2012/03/71_475x317.jpg)

Oilcloth is ideal for this very useful travel companion. Using oilcloth or water-resistant fabric makes a DIY changing pad durable and easy to wipe clean. It folds flat for travel and fits conveniently inside a diaper bag or backpack during long road trips.

[Tutorial](https://www.prettyprudent.com/diy-travel-changing-pad-2/)

## 8. Hair Iron Travel Case

![Hair Iron Travel Case](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2012/03/81-1024x699_1024x699.jpg)

Having the wire of your curling or flat iron all tangled up with your earphones or charger is a nightmare. Sewing a heat-resistant travel pouch protects your styling tools, keeps power cords contained neatly, and helps you keep your cool while packing.

[Tutorial](https://craftbuds.com/flat-ironcurling-iron-travel-case/)

## 9. Passport and Card Holder

![Passport and Card Holder](https://img.allw.mn/content/travel/2012/03/9_550x358.gif)

Doesn't the leather look beautiful? There are tutorials out there for fabric passport holders but I think this leather one is too lovely not to include in this list of **travel DIY projects**. Crafting your own sleeve with slots for credit cards and boarding passes ensures important documents stay organized. Discover more inspiring crafting ideas on [AllWomenStalk](https://allwomenstalk.com/).

[Tutorial](http://archive.poppytalk.com/2011/07/how-to-make-your-own-leather-passport.html)

## 10. Sleep Mask

_ For long flights, a sleep mask is a must. A padded satin or cotton sleep mask blocks out unwanted light on long flights or in bright hotel rooms. Customizing your sleep mask with embroidery or lace adds a personal touch for comfortable rest while traveling.
