# 7 Best Relaxing Honeymoon Destinations for a Peaceful Getaway

After all the planning, endless checklists, and running around that a wedding involves, your honeymoon is the ultimate time to relax, leave all your wedding planning stress behind, and begin your new chapter together. Lianne Crosby shares several quiet, relaxing honeymoon destinations where newlywed couples can recharge in complete serenity.

A honeymoon is a special opportunity for newlywed couples to enjoy the first moments of their lives together, and there is no better way to celebrate than by escaping to a scenic, peaceful honeymoon destination. Whether you dream of white sandy beaches or secluded safari lodges, explore these handpicked spots to find your ideal getaway. You can also visit [Hitched.co](https://goo.gl/5PYrtY) for additional wedding inspiration and honeymoon ideas.

## 1. Antigua

![Antigua](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/vo/rk/bjy4eokl_600x400.jpg)

Antigua is often celebrated as one of the world's most romantic resort islands, thanks to its breathtaking mix of Caribbean charm and serene coastal beauty. Dickenson Bay stands out as one of the most famous beach areas, featuring lush seaside garden oases filled with majestic palm trees, winding pathways, and turquoise waters. For more travel details, check the [official Antigua & Barbuda Tourism](https://www.visitantiguabarbuda.com/) guide.

You can also consider staying in an all-suite village overlooking the coastline, where scenic views of draping hillsides take your breath away right from your private seaside villa. It is a dream location for newlyweds looking for the best quiet honeymoon destinations, ensuring a trip you will treasure forever. If you prefer a coastal island alternative in Africa later on your trip, compare this with [Seychelles](#6).

**Highlights of an Antigua Honeymoon:**

- 365 distinct white sand beaches to explore
- Unmatched sunset views along Dickenson Bay
- Luxurious private seaside villas for couples

## 2. Mauritius

![Mauritius](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/gt/oq/ugnuftl9_600x400.jpg)

Mauritius is another stunning honeymoon destination located 2,000 kilometers off the southeast coast of the African continent. With its pristine white sandy beaches and crystal-clear ocean waters, it is a tropical paradise that couples fall in love with instantly. Learn more at the [official Mauritius Tourism site](https://www.mymauritius.travel/).

You will be able to spend your days relaxing on quiet beaches or beside calm infinity pools, watching vibrant sunsets fill the evening sky. Take a refreshing dip in the cooling Tamarind Falls or experience the quiet majesty of Ganga Talao, a sacred lake set within an extinct volcanic crater. You can also sample rich local flavors by visiting bustling markets and fragrant spice stalls. Mauritius offers a peaceful honeymoon experience that you will be reminiscing about with family and friends for decades.

## 3. Tofo, Mozambique

![Tofo, Mozambique](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/vw/cs/ilk4t4qd_600x398.jpg)

The restful coastal town of Tofo might not be at the top of every traditional travel list, but it remains one of the best hidden gems for a quiet honeymoon destination. If you enjoy serene coastlines, you will find it just as charming as [Belo Sur Mer in Madagascar](#7).

The village preserves a historic coastal charm with its tranquil bay overlooking the warm waters of the Indian Ocean. You and your partner will adore the laid-back atmosphere, welcoming community, and delicious freshly caught grilled lobster served right on the beach.

For couples seeking a touch of ocean adventure alongside relaxation, Mozambique is world-renowned for swimming with ocean giants like manta rays and whale sharks. While the area attracts visitors from across the globe, it is easy to find secluded stretches of sand where you can escape the crowds and enjoy quiet, peaceful moments together.

## 4. Tunisia

![Tunisia](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/vg/qr/0kedgn7a_500x333.jpg)

The Tunisian desert is a surprisingly romantic Tunisia honeymoon destination, offering starry night skies and breathtaking golden dunes. A memorable place to begin your stay is at Yadis Ksar Ghilane, a luxury desert resort where you can sleep under the stars in a palm-lined oasis.

Another fantastic option is exploring Douz, known as the gateway to the Sahara. From here, couples can embark on excursions to Jebil National Park or take thrilling off-road desert trips across the sweeping Saharan sands for an unforgettable mix of relaxation and adventure.

## 5. Masai Mara National Park, Kenya

![Masai Mara National Park, Kenya](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ky/bj/0jrar0u6_600x420.jpg)

If you are feeling adventurous, consider a getaway to Kenya's Masai Mara National Reserve. Home to incredible African wildlife and sweeping savannas, it offers a truly unique backdrop for newlyweds. Plan your safari through the [official Kenya Tourism board](https://www.magicalkenya.com/).

Beyond game drives, you will have the chance to experience rich local cultures and warm hospitality. Choose a luxury tented bush camp where you can unwind by the river, take an al fresco shower under the golden sunset, and enjoy peaceful evenings under starry skies.

**Why Choose a Safari Honeymoon:**

- Spectacular African sunset views over the savanna
- Intimate luxury tented accommodation near wildlife
- Unforgettable stargazing far from city lights

## 6. Praslin Island, Seychelles

![Praslin Island, Seychelles](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/bq/c0/oylprx1k_600x397.jpg)

While many couples searching for a relaxing beach resort honeymoon automatically look toward classic spots in the South Pacific or Caribbean like [Antigua](#1), Praslin Island in Seychelles is a breathtaking alternative. Explore travel advice at the [official Seychelles Tourism portal](https://www.seychelles.travel/).

Praslin Island remains peaceful and uncrowded, featuring virtually untouched tropical beaches such as Grand Anse. With its soft golden sands, granite rock formations, and clear turquoise waters, Praslin Island provides a romantic, relaxing honeymoon experience that feels like your own private sanctuary.

## 7. Belo Sur Mer, Madagascar

![Belo Sur Mer, Madagascar](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/l4/ws/lx4o53vv_600x402.jpg)

The island of Madagascar features remarkably isolated, romantic coastlines with golden sands and crystal-clear turquoise waters. Belo Sur Mer is a quiet fishing village where locals prepare fresh, delicious seafood right along the shore.

You can venture inland to explore Madagascar's national parks and famous wildlife, then return to the coast to end your day with a tranquil sunset stroll along the beach. It is an ideal escape for couples seeking a quiet honeymoon destination away from typical tourist crowds.
