# What Happens When Tennis Takes Over The Plaza...

[Lacoste](https://www.instagram.com/lacoste/) has infused The Plaza Hotel in New York with its vibrant spirit for a tennis takeover like no other. As the city bustles with energy during the season's final Grand Slam, Lacoste has turned this iconic venue into Club Lacoste New York. The event offers exclusive experiences that blend the heritage of tennis with modern luxury. From August to September 5, guests have the chance to immerse themselves in a world where style and sport elegantly converge. Whether you’re a tennis aficionado or a fashion enthusiast, this unique gathering promises an unforgettable taste of Lacoste's signature flair.

## An Iconic Entrance

The entrance to The Plaza has been transformed with a signature Lacoste touch. Visitors are greeted by a green carpet emblazoned with the iconic crocodile logo, setting the tone for a sophisticated tennis escape. This detail not only enhances the opulence of The Plaza but also emphasizes Lacoste's commitment to elegance. A visit to The Plaza now feels like stepping onto the grounds of a grand tennis club, inviting attendees to experience the blend of tradition and fashion that has long been Lacoste's hallmark. The ambiance immediately engages guests and elevates their expectations for the event.

## A Spectacular Venue

Inside, The Plaza's grandeur is on full display, with décor inspired by classic tennis elements. The blend of crystal chandeliers and lush greenery against tennis net installations creates a striking visual impact. The iconic New York landmark provides a rich backdrop that complements Lacoste's aesthetic, offering guests an extravagant yet familiar environment. This venue choice amplifies the ambiance of Club Lacoste, bridging the gap between historical elegance and contemporary design. The setting is not just visually captivating; it provides a storied atmosphere that enhances the brand's rich heritage.

## On-Court Experiences

![image](https://img.allw.mn/uploads/39be97a9-2314-489e-a58d-f7735c48a3be_1638x2047.jpg)

Photo: [Lacoste](https://www.instagram.com/lacoste/)

Club Lacoste New York is more than just a visual treat. It allows guests to engage in tennis activities that bring them close to the sport’s roots. A pop-up court inside The Plaza invites guests to participate in or watch impromptu matches. It is a reminder of Lacoste's origin in the tennis world, named after its founder, French tennis legend René Lacoste. This feature provides a dynamic experience, perfectly blending activity with ambiance, inviting guests to interact with the brand in a unique way. It's an experience that emphasizes Lacoste's dedication to the sport and its community.

This carefully curated event has highlighted Lacoste's unique ability to blend sports with sophisticated style. For those fortunate enough to attend, the event offers a peek into the world of Lacoste, where every detail tells a story, and every moment is a testament to the brand's enduring elegance and sporty charm.
