# 8 of the Best Adventure Holidays in the World for Thrill\-Seekers

To some, adventure holidays are better than the coveted island breakaway. Adventure holidays are a way to explore the world around you, without trawling malls, staying in 5 star luxury hotels and having endless massages at a spa. Although relaxing by the pool is indeed a nice way of spending your downtime, the **best adventure holidays in the world** are designed for travelers who crave memorable experiences, outdoor challenges, and genuine connection with nature. Whether you are looking for high-altitude trekking, ocean paddling, or wildlife encounters, planning **worldwide adventure holidays** can redefine how you experience new cultures and landscapes. Here are 8 of the World’s Best Adventure Holidays to choose from for your next journey.

## 1. Hiking in the Himalayas

![Hiking in the Himalayas](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2012/09/1_hiking-in-the-himalayas_600x375.jpg)

Most people think that hiking anywhere in the notorious Everest region is strictly for hardcore, super fit, and experienced hikers. But there are accessible trek routes for travelers of all experience levels eager to admire the towering peaks of the Himalayas. Combine a fascinating trip through the exotic town of Kathmandu, explore ancient Hindu temples and Buddhist shrines, and soak up the diversity of the bustling markets before heading out to the peaks of the Himalayas. You’ll sleep in darling little tea houses along the way and be one of the most unforgettable **adventure holidays worldwide**.

If you prefer soaring above cold landscapes after taking on mountain paths, you can also look into a [polar balloon flight](#6) for a totally unique perspective from above.

## 2. Cycling in El Camino, Spain

![Cycling in El Camino, Spain](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2012/09/2_cycling-in-el-camino-spain_600x400.jpg)

Possibly one of the most epic cycling trails in the world, starts at Roncesvalles, along the Camino de Santiago and going all the way along the 783kms route to the Cathedral of Santiago. It has long been a legendary pilgrimage route. For more than 1,000 years pilgrims took this route that goes through the Cathedral city of Leon and winds through some of the most incredible ancient villages and historic towns in Northern Spain. These days, tourists looking for the ultimate **worldwide adventure holidays**, take this trip over 8 days, staying in the small towns along the way. Check details on route planning via [Spain's official tourism portal](https://www.spain.info) before setting off.

Here are key essentials to keep in mind when prepping for a multi-day cycle trip:

- Choose a durable touring bicycle or all-terrain gravel bike suited for mixed surfaces.
- Pack lightweight, moisture-wicking layers for changing mountain weather conditions.
- Secure your credentials for pilgrim stamps at village stops along the route.

## 3. Antarctic Voyage

![Antarctic Voyage](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2012/09/3_antarctic-voyage_600x399.jpg)

An expedition to Antarctica has got to be one of the most incredible **best adventure holidays in the world**. Book a trip on one of the grand expedition ships, like those operated by [Hurtigruten Expeditions](https://www.hurtigruten.com), which takes visitors on tours from Tierra Del Fuego, stopping off to visit the resplendent Ushuaia National Park, passing through the Drake Passage, before arriving in the breath taking winter wonderland of Antarctica. The 10 day voyage explores the planet’s last virtually untouched landscapes and visitors will be able to experience the most incredible marine life such as whales, penguins and seals, as well as listen to the fascinating talks about Antarctica, the legends and the mystery of this incredible, barren landscape.

## 4. Cowboy Adventures in Canada

![Cowboy Adventures in Canada](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2012/09/4_cowboy-adventures-in-canada_600x400.jpg)

Embrace your inner rancher and head out for exciting **world adventure holidays** to Alberta in Canada. Get back to nature and experience the beauty of the wild west, as it has been carefully and lovingly preserved at some of the state’s ranches. Follow overnight horse trails through Kananaskis Country and through Bow Valley, go white water rafting and zip line through the fir plantations. Go on a real cowboy wagon trip and enjoy the ultimate cowboy adventure experience with fantastic, friendly teams in one of the most beautiful places in the world. If water-based expeditions appeal to you as much as trail riding, consider [navigating limestone coves](#5) on your next trip.

## 5. Kayaking in Halong Bay, Vietnam

![Kayaking in Halong Bay, Vietnam](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2012/09/5_kayaking-in-halong-bay-vietnam_600x424.jpg)

Halong Bay has got to be one of the most incredible landscapes in the world, where time appears to stand completely still – the perfect destination for some terrific adventure tours. The mesmerising deep emerald waters, and the swirling mist, give way to the mysterious and eerie limestone outcrops that appear to rise from the depths of the bay, cropping up almost appearing to be scattered everywhere. A guided kayak tour will lead you to all the secret, hidden coves, amazing caves and sensational views. Glide through the magical waters and wave to passing junks and experience serenity like nowhere else on earth.

## 6. Hot Air Balloon Tour in the North Pole

![Hot Air Balloon Tour in the North Pole](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2012/09/6_hot-air-balloon-tour-in-the-north-pole_600x400.jpg)

This new tour is one of a kind, and can without a doubt holds its own at the very front of the queue when it comes to looking for the **best adventure holidays** anywhere in the world. Part of a 2 week trip, starting off on the 50 Years of Victory, an icebreaker ship, you will have a week to experience the exotic landscapes as you make your way to the North Pole. Crack the champers on arrival and head out to the launch pad, where your hot air balloon will lift you high into the sky above the ice. Expect to be rather chilly, and completely in awe.

## 7. Semi-Sub Tour of the Great Barrier Reef

![Semi-Sub Tour of the Great Barrier Reef](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2012/09/7_semi-sub-tour-of-the-great-barrier-reef_600x398.jpg)

The Great Barrier Reef is one of the only natural phenomena that can be seen from space. A world heritage site protected in part by the [Great Barrier Reef Marine Park Authority](https://www.gbrmpa.gov.au), this astounding coral reef attracts thousands of visitors every year, who flock to the reef to explore the underwater palaces, the incredible marine life, experience dolphin encounters, snorkel, dive and swim in and around the sparkling azure waters around the great reef. A semi sub tour is the latest in adventure tours; the air conditioned sub allows visitors to explore another world, without getting wet at all.

For those eager to encounter even more sea life after swimming near coral reefs, an [antarctic voyage](#3) lets you observe whales and seals up close in polar waters.

## 8. Safari in Uganda

![Safari in Uganda](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2012/09/8_safari-in-uganda_600x450.jpg)

We are all familiar with South Africana and Tanzanian safaris but for wildlife adventure tours it’s hard to beat the Uganda experience, managed with support from the [Uganda Wildlife Authority](https://ugandawildlife.org). Track chimpanzees and monkeys through the Chambura Gorge and look for hippo, crocodiles and exotic birdlife on a boat trip in the Kazinga Channel. The highlight of Uganda adventure holidays has to be the trek through the rainforest to see mountain gorillas in the Bwindi National Park.

Adventure holidays don’t have to break the bank or be too strenuous, and getting out to enjoy adventure tours, will surely be the most memorable holidays of all.
