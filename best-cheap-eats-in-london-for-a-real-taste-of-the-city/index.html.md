# 9 Best Cheap Eats in London: The Ultimate Budget Dining Guide

The UK capital is known to be expensive for tourists if you’re not careful about getting the most bang for your buck, but finding the top cheap eats in London is easier than you think—it’s just a case of knowing where to look. While you might occasionally bag a brilliant discount or coupon for a taster menu at a Michelin-star spot, sustaining your entire trip on a budget requires reliable everyday gems. From street food stalls in Soho to legendary spots for cheap eats in Kings Cross and Shoreditch, eating well in London doesn't have to break the bank. Here is your ultimate guide to the best cheap eats in London for a real taste of the city without compromising on flavor.

To help you navigate the city's budget dining scene across different neighborhoods, here is a quick overview of top value spots:

| Neighborhood | Spot | Specialty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soho | Yalla Yalla | Lebanese Meze & Street Food |
| Finsbury Park | Jai Khrishna | Vegetarian Indian Curries |
| Chiswick | Franco Manca | Sourdough Neapolitan Pizza |

Below, we explore each of these iconic budget locations in detail so you can plan your culinary route through the city.

## 1. Yalla Yalla, Soho

![Yalla Yalla, Soho](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2013/07/1_yalla-yalla-soho1_596x350.jpg)

Middle Eastern food is immensely popular across the capital, and the social side of sharing dishes makes it a fantastic option for group dining. There’s also the added bonus that meze-type meals offer exceptional value, making them some of the best cheap eats in Soho London. Tucked away on Winsley Street in Soho, just steps from the bustle of Oxford Street and Theatreland, Yalla Yalla serves up authentic Beirut street food. It’s a cozy, no-frills restaurant where you can enjoy a veritable feast of chicken taouk, warm halloumi, fresh tabbouleh, and creamy hummus for around £15 ($23) per person. If you're looking for cheap eats soho london has to offer, this vibrant spot should be high on your list.

## 2. Jai Khrishna, Finsbury Park

![Jai Khrishna, Finsbury Park](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2013/07/2_jai-khrishna-finsbury-park_550x412.jpg)

Some of the cheapest places to eat in London operate on a BYOB (Bring Your Own Bottle) policy, which helps diners save significantly on drink bills while allowing restaurateurs to offer unbeatable prices. At Jai Khrishna in Stroud Green Road near Finsbury Park, you can pair a supermarket budget wine with delicious, home-style Indian cuisine. Known as a staple for cheap eats in Finsbury Park, their hearty vegetable curries, thalis, and crispy samosas start at just £3 to £5. If you're exploring North London and searching for Jai Khrishna Finsbury Park or similar vegetarian hotspots, this unpretentious eatery delivers incredible authentic flavor for minimal spend.

## 3. Franco Manca, Chiswick

![Franco Manca, Chiswick](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2013/07/3_franco-manca-chiswick_600x424.jpg)

No list of budget-friendly dining would be complete without a standout pizzeria. [Franco Manca](https://www.francomanca.co.uk/) in Chiswick has grown into a true institution across London, pioneering slow-cooked sourdough pizza baked in traditional wood-fired brick ovens built by Neapolitan artisans. Watching your pizza rise and blister in minutes is part of the experience. Paired with handmade lemonade or organic house wine, a fresh sourdough pizza here remains one of the best cheap eats Chiswick has to offer, with classic options coming in well under £10.

## 4. Song Que, Shoreditch

![Song Que, Shoreditch](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2013/07/4_song-que-shoreditch_600x400.jpg)

London boasts a thriving Asian culinary scene, and Kingsland Road in Shoreditch is famously lined with vibrant Vietnamese eateries. Local food lovers consistently point to [Song Que Cafe](http://www.songque.co.uk/) as the standout choice among them. High on authentic flavor and generous portions, Song Que is widely regarded as one of the best cheap eats Shoreditch High Street offers. Their signature stewed beef pho, fragrant banh mi, and fresh summer rolls pack incredible depth of flavor at wallet-friendly prices, making it a must-visit when hunting for the cheapest eats in London.

## 5. Fryer’s Delight, Kings Cross

![Fryer’s Delight, Kings Cross](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2013/07/5_fryers-delight-kings-cross_600x450.jpg)

If you prefer traditional British comfort food over international fare, classic fish and chips is essential. Interestingly, one of the top spots for cheap eats in Kings Cross and Holborn is Fryer's Delight, a traditional chippy run by an Italian family for decades. Cab drivers and locals line up for thick-cut cod and fluffy chips cooked the classic way in beef dripping. It's a vintage, retro-style establishment where you can enjoy traditional British fare, and its BYOB option keeps your meal extremely economical compared to standard city restaurants.

When dining on a budget in London, keeping a few smart strategies in mind can stretch your pound even further:

- **Look for BYOB places:** Bringing your own drinks saves significantly on restaurant markups.

- **Explore neighborhood markets:** Local food markets often serve fresh lunch meals for under £10.

- **Opt for cross-town favorites:** Spots like [Yalla Yalla](#1) in Soho or [Franco Manca](#3) offer consistent quality across branches.

## 6. Chilango, the City

![Chilango, the City](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2013/07/6_chilango-the-city_600x399.jpg)

Featuring an open kitchen setup where you customize your burritos, tacos, or salad bowls, [Chilango](https://chilango.co.uk/) brings lively Mexican flavors to London's financial district. The vibrant, modern décor creates an energetic setting whether you dine in or grab takeout. Portions are famously generous—their massive burritos stuffed with slow-cooked meats, black beans, guacamole, and fresh salsas easily satisfy big appetites for around £10 ($15), making it a top pick for quick, affordable dining in the City.

## 7. Red Sea Restaurant, Shepherd's Bush

![Red Sea Restaurant, Shepherd's Bush](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2013/07/7_red-sea-restaurant-shepherds-bush_600x480.jpg)

Serving up an eclectic blend of East African cuisine with warm hospitality, Red Sea Restaurant in Shepherd's Bush is a hidden gem you’ll want to revisit. The setting is unpretentious and homely, but the expansive menu features reasonably priced dishes showcasing flavors from Ethiopia, Somalia, Eritrea, and Yemen. From rich lamb stews served with injera bread to fragrant spiced rice dishes, main courses range from £5 to £12 ($8–$18). If you're searching for cheap eats Shepherd's Bush locals love, Red Sea Restaurant Shepherd's Bush is a memorable choice, especially when paired with their signature fresh lemon milkshakes.

## 8. Roti Joupa, Clapham

![Roti Joupa, Clapham](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2013/07/8_roti-joupa-clapham_600x448.jpg)

For authentic Caribbean flavors in South London, Roti Joupa in Clapham is legendary. Specializing in Trinidadian street food like soft, flaky rotis filled with curried goat, chicken, or channa (chickpeas), as well as mouthwatering doubles, this takeaway delivers incredible flavor. Located just a short stroll from Clapham Common, it's perfect for picking up lunch to enjoy outdoors on a sunny afternoon. Standard portions start around £5 ($8), proving that high quality cheap eats in Clapham are well within reach. Whether you're looking up Roti Joupa Shepherd's Bush or Clapham locations, their Trinidadian recipes are second to none.

## 9. Any Greasy Spoon, All over London

![Any Greasy Spoon, All over London](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2013/07/9_any-greasy-spoon-all-over-london_600x450.jpg)

If you need rib-sticking fuel to sustain a full day of sightseeing, classic London greasy spoon cafes offer unbeatable value. These traditional neighbourhood cafes serve up generous Full English breakfasts complete with eggs, bacon, sausages, baked beans, mushrooms, grilled tomatoes, hash browns, and buttered toast alongside steaming mugs of builder's tea—typically for £5 to £8 ($7–$11). It's one of the best cheap food in London experiences you can have, offering a cozy slice of everyday British culture in virtually every borough.

This is just a selection of the best cheap eats in London. The variety across the city is remarkable, representing almost every world cuisine imaginable. Whether you are exploring Central London or residential neighborhoods, delicious budget meals are always nearby. Where is your favorite cheap eatery in London?
