# 7 Stunning Cities in Provence, France You Need to Visit

When my host family from Switzerland took me on a getaway across Southern France, I had the incredible chance to explore the most captivating cities in Provence, France. Known world-wide for its sun-drenched lavender fields, olive groves, and breathtaking Mediterranean landscapes, Provence is filled with unforgettable destinations. I was fortunate enough to visit many of these standout provence cities twice during my stay. If you are wondering what cities are in Provence France that deserve a spot on your travel itinerary, this list highlights seven distinct spots you should definitely check out on your next trip.

## 1. St. Tropez

![St. Tropez](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/gr/y4/qh/cko29lr8_600x391.jpg)

As a dazzling coastal highlight of the French Riviera, St. Tropez is a glamorous port town famous for its luxury yachts, boutique shopping, and cobble-paved old town streets. The narrow roadways weave gracefully through the town center, leading toward bustling cafes and oceanfront vistas. You can check out the [official St. Tropez Tourism Guide](https://www.sainttropeztourisme.com/en/) for local event schedules and harbour boat tour itineraries. During our visit, we took a scenic boat tour past waterfront villas like Villa Heineken and even spotted celebrities like Elton John and Lady Gaga strolling along the docks.

## 2. Le Lavandou

![Le Lavandou](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/dl/ct/00/iqnwgwef_540x356.jpg)

Positioned right along the Mediterranean coast, Le Lavandou offers a relaxed beach atmosphere that provides a peaceful contrast to coastal hubs like [Nice](#6). Its twelve fine-sand beaches are perfect for soaking up the warm Mediterranean sun, while active travelers can enjoy wind-sailing, paddleboarding, and parasailing. The town frequently hosts free beach concerts during summer evenings, where I was lucky enough to see French singer Olivia Ruiz perform live under the stars. Don't forget to browse the local flea market for handcrafted souvenirs and regional produce.

## 3. Les Baux

![Les Baux](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/bi/rf/tu/h4qaapd7_600x375.jpg)

Nestled atop the Alpilles mountains, Les Baux-de-Provence is a cliffside village rich in medieval history, charming artisan boutiques, and stone-paved pedestrian walkways. The elevated position gives visitors panoramic views across the valley below. Driving along the scenic mountain roads surrounding the village reveals incredible viewpoints for photography. During our drive, we even spotted a newlywed couple capturing their wedding photos against the dramatic rock formations.

Here are a few highlights to enjoy while visiting Les Baux:

- Walk through the restored medieval fortress grounds at the top of the village.
- Explore unique craft shops selling olive oil, lavender products, and pottery.
- Take in panoramic photos from mountain lookouts along the surrounding roads.

## 4. Aix-En-Provence

![Aix-En-Provence](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/b0/ql/up/bgz55ewb_600x397.jpg)

Serving as the historic cultural heart of the region, Aix-en-Provence combines vibrant university town energy with classic Provencal elegance. If you enjoy historic villages like [Les Baux](#3), Aix offers a grander urban feel filled with grand avenues, mossy fountains, and outdoor cafes. You can discover local walking tours on the [official Aix-en-Provence Tourism site](https://www.aixenprovencetourism.com/en/). From browsing daily street markets along Cours Mirabeau to visiting world-class art museums, Aix seamlessly balances its rich heritage with modern vitality.

## 5. Roussillon

![Roussillon](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/r7/px/qm/gd5rzs8g_600x316.jpg)

Famous for its striking red and yellow ochre cliffs, Roussillon looks like something painted directly onto a canvas. Warm terracotta hues color both the village homes and the surrounding cliffs. Much like the artistic legacy found in [St. Rémy](#7), Roussillon inspires nature lovers and artists alike. A mutual friend guided us along the Sentier des Ocres hiking trail, where the vibrant red earth creates a stunning visual contrast against the deep green pine trees. It is truly one of the most distinctive scenic stops in the region.

## 6. Nice

![Nice](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/q4/ou/ow/wg25yi79_600x338.jpg)

Nice is a dynamic city where seaside relaxation meets vibrant urban culture. The northern districts feature broad avenues and impressive landmarks like the St. Nicholas Russian Orthodox Cathedral. Moving south toward the coast, the architecture shifts to Mediterranean pastel facades along the famous Promenade des Anglais. You can plan city passes and transit using the [official Nice Côte d'Azur Tourism portal](https://explorenicecotedazur.org/en/). The city perfectly bridges urban excitement with relaxed Riviera beach life.

Top things to experience during your stay in Nice include:

- Stroll down the iconic Promenade des Anglais along the Baie des Anges.
- Visit the historic St. Nicholas Russian Orthodox Cathedral.
- Wander through the colorful flower market in Vieux Nice (Old Town).

## 7. St. Rémy

![St. Rémy](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/xa/gx/i9/xg0kwdjd_600x330.jpg)

Saint-Rémy-de-Provence is an idyllic town steeped in artistic history and timeless French charm. Vincent van Gogh famously spent time here, capturing the surrounding landscape in iconic works such as Starry Night. The village has long attracted notable residents, including Princess Caroline of Monaco. You can check out historical landmarks through the [official Saint-Rémy Tourism site](https://www.saintremy-de-provence.com/en/). Whether you prefer luxury retreats like [St. Tropez](#1) or quiet countryside villages, Saint-Rémy offers quiet squares, welcoming cafes, and the beautiful Church of Saint-Martin right in the town center.
