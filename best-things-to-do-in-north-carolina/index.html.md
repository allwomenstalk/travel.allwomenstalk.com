# 8 Best Things to Do in North Carolina: Ultimate Travel Guide

With a myriad of unforgettable things to do in North Carolina, it remains one of the most rewarding vacation destinations on the East Coast. Known affectionately as the Tar Heel State, North Carolina offers an incredible variety of landscapes and experiences. You can spend your morning along the refreshing Atlantic coastline, head west to partake in outdoor activities across the rugged Blue Ridge countryside, or immerse yourself in vibrant local arts and music scenes. Whether you are looking for outdoor adventure or historic coastal charm, here is a curated guide to the 8 best things to do in North Carolina for an unforgettable trip.

## 1. Grandfather Mountain

No visit to North Carolina would be complete without experiencing the majestic 5,946-foot Grandfather Mountain. Located in the northwestern part of the state near Linville, this towering peak offers sweeping 360-degree views from the famous Mile High Swinging Bridge. As you traverse craggy trails and scenic alpine paths, keep your eyes peeled for wildlife, including native black bears, river otters, and majestic golden eagles. For outdoor enthusiasts seeking top-tier hiking and mountain vistas, visiting [Grandfather Mountain](https://grandfather.com/) is truly one of the essential things to see in North Carolina. If you enjoy dramatic mountain scenery, you might also like exploring [Catawba River canoeing](#3) for another natural adventure in the state.

## 2. Elizabethan Gardens

One of the quietest and most scenic places to visit in North Carolina is the Elizabethan Gardens in Manteo on Roanoke Island. Designed by renowned landscape architects Innocenti and Webel, these lush gardens were created as a living memorial to the first English colonists who landed in America. Featuring authentic 16th-century style flora, historic marble statues, and quiet shaded pathways, a leisurely walk through these tranquil grounds is a wonderful way to unwind. Visitors can also take the Historic Albemarle Tour, which links several coastal heritage attractions across eastern North Carolina.

## 3. Catawba River Canoeing

For travelers looking to get their heart racing, an active trip down the scenic Catawba River provides plenty of excitement. Paddling down clear waters and drifting past the wooded banks of rural Carolina offers a magnificent perspective on the state's natural beauty. Guided canoeing and kayaking excursions cater to both beginners and experienced paddlers, making this water activity accessible to everyone. If you love spending time on the water, be sure to also check out [surfing on the Atlantic coast](#4).

## 4. Go Surfing!

Relaxing on sandy beaches is wonderful, but if you are craving something more active on your trip, taking a surfing lesson along the Outer Banks or Wrightsville Beach is an incredible experience. North Carolina's barrier islands feature some of the most consistent surf on the East Coast. Local surf schools with certified instructors offer lessons for all skill levels. There is nothing quite like catching a wave as the Atlantic sun sets over the shoreline, making this one of the top outdoor activities in North Carolina.

Here are a few quick tips for planning your coastal surf session:

- Choose morning sessions for calmer ocean conditions and lighter winds.
- Rent gear locally at coastal surf shops in Nags Head or Wrightsville.
- Always check local tide charts and beach safety flags before heading out.

## 5. NASCAR Kings

For fans of motorsports and American sports culture, a trip to North Carolina's racing heartland is a must. The state is steeped in stock car racing history, highlighted by the legacy of the Petty family in Level Cross and the legendary [NASCAR Hall of Fame](https://www.nascarhall.com/) in Charlotte. Celebrating champions like 7-time Winston Cup winner Richard Petty, these attractions exhibit iconic race cars, trophy collections, and interactive displays that chart the evolution of American racing.

## 6. Carolina Chocolate Drops

Music lovers should explore the rich musical heritage of the Tar Heel State, exemplified by the Grammy Award-winning string band Carolina Chocolate Drops. Formed in Durham, North Carolina, this acclaimed group brought traditional African-American string band and fiddle music back into the national spotlight. Although members have pursued individual solo careers and projects over the years, their foundational albums and live performances celebrate the deep cultural history of North Carolina's Piedmont region.

## 7. River Arts District

For arts and culture, head to Asheville's famous [River Arts District](https://www.riverartsdistrict.com/) along the French Broad River. Housed in converted historic industrial warehouses, over 200 working artists create and display their creations across dozens of studios. Visitors can walk through galleries, observe artists at work—from pottery and glassblowing to painting and woodworking—and purchase unique handcrafted souvenirs directly from local creators.

## 8. USS North Carolina

Combine coastal sightseeing with historic exploration by visiting Wilmington, home of the World War II battleship [USS Battleship North Carolina](https://www.battleshipnc.com/). Docked across the river from downtown Wilmington, this decorated warship earned 15 battle stars across every major Pacific naval offensive in World War II. Visitors can take self-guided tours exploring nine decks, mess halls, gun turrets, and crew quarters where 2,500 sailors lived. Stepping out onto the main deck offers fantastic scenic views across the Wilmington waterfront, making it one of the most photogenic historic landmarks in the state.

As one of the most geographically and culturally diverse states on the Atlantic seaboard, North Carolina offers an endless array of travel experiences. From high mountain bridges and rapid rivers to historic gardens, art districts, and coastal battleships, these top 8 attractions are just the beginning of what there is to see and do.
