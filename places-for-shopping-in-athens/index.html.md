# 7 Best Places for Shopping in Athens: Outlets, Markets & Luxury Spots

Shopping in Athens is a wonderful experience, not only because we’re talking about a modern capital that has everything you could possibly ask for, but also because shopping and loving every minute of it seems to be an authentic local tradition. From what I’ve seen, locals love to shop, and being surrounded by so many vibrant boutiques and fellow shopaholics, cornered by friendly sales assistants, and tempted by endless sales, I quickly relaxed into the retail therapy. If you are looking for the best places to shop in Athens, you are in for a treat! Whether you want to score deals at an [athens outlet mall](#2), hunt for vintage treasures, or explore high-end luxury fashion in the main [athens shopping district](#4), after you find yourself here during one of the crazy everything-must-go sales, you’ll fall in love with the experience too. I’ll skip Ermou today because I’ve already discussed it in previous posts, and focus on other incredible places to shop in Athens:

## 1. Arthur Glenn Shopping Village

From high-end designer brands like Dolce & Gabbana or Roberto Cavalli to chic yet affordable favorites like Nine West, this shopping destination sure has it all. That means you definitely need to check it out regardless of your (or your credit card’s) spending capacity! Known locally as [McArthurGlen Designer Outlet Athens](https://www.designeroutletathens.gr/) (or Designer Outlet Athens), this charming open-air village is located very close to the airport and the Athens Zoo, so you’ll have no problem finding it. Once you arrive, just park in the basement, hop inside the elevator, and step right into a fabulous world of shops with year-round discounts. If you drop by during standard end-of-season sales (August and February are prime times), you’ll get to enjoy even deeper discounts—a sale-on-sale phenomenon I personally love! Outlet shopping in Athens is so much fun, and this modern complex is easily one of the top spots to visit.

## 2. Factory Outlet

The [Factory Outlet](https://www.factoryoutlet.gr/) is another major reason why I love shopping in Athens so much, and if I really had to pick a favorite location, I’d advise you to visit the flagship store near Faliro. From everyday streetwear brands like Guess, Miss Sixty, Diesel, and Replay to high-end names you usually spot on Net-a-Porter, this outlet mall is packed with delicious items that could break even Chuck Norris’ bank! During my last visit, I snagged a pair of fab white Replay wedges for 15 Euros (which retailed for 150!), Miss Sixty heels for 40 Euros (originally over 150), and a pair of gorgeous, strappy Zanotti sandals for 80 Euros. My fanatic shopping spree had to stop there because I couldn’t find another excuse to buy more! The best part? While browsing through Miss Sixty shoes in late January, I found current fall/winter collection styles priced far better than standard store sale prices.

- **Top Brands:** Guess, Replay, Diesel, Miss Sixty, Giuseppe Zanotti
- **Best Time to Visit:** End of January and late August for maximum markdowns

## 3. Flea Market

Most regular shops don’t open on Sundays in Greece, but that doesn’t mean you can’t have a fun morning hunting for bargains in Monastiraki! Everyone who has something to offer gathers here on Sunday mornings. Some vendors sell quirky vintage items, while others offer genuine hidden gems—it’s definitely worth exploring because you can run into just about anything. If you’re interested in thrifting in Athens or picking up unique souvenirs, Adrianou Street is the exact place you want to be walking down on weekends. If you show up early enough, you can even find vintage antiques or a perfectly good bicycle! Once you get tired of haggling or sifting through piles of old treasures, you can grab a bite to eat or sit down for a Greek coffee nearby while people-watching. Afterward, you can easily walk over to check out [fresh local delicacies](#6) nearby.

## 4. Voukourestiou Street

Luxury shopping in Athens isn’t complete without a stroll down Voukourestiou Street, one of the most glamorous and expensive streets in the city. Located just a short five-minute walk from Syntagma Square, it won’t take much of your precious sightseeing time to explore. High-end fashion houses like Mulberry, Louis Vuitton, Hermès, Dior, and Prada line this elegant pedestrian street. If a brand-new designer handbag is on your wishlist for high end shopping in Athens, rest assured you will find the flagship collections right here in this stylish shopping area.

## 5. Swap Shop

If you love pre-loved luxury, [Swap Shop](https://swapshop.gr/) might easily be one of the premier designer consignment boutiques for an athens consignment sale experience. If you enjoy knowing your designer clothes, shoes, or handbags had a stylish life before you, this is definitely the place to check out! Who knows—you might even find that sold-out holy grail bag you’ve been searching for over months. And if you happen to drop by during seasonal sales, you’ll score even better deals! Be sure to check their official site for current opening hours and inventory updates before visiting.

| Monastiraki & Swap Shop | Vintage Finds & Consignment | Bargain Prices to Pre-Loved Luxury |

## 6. Central Market

If your idea of places to shop in Athens involves authentic olives, aromatic Greek spices, artisanal cheeses, and traditional delicacies, prepare for an exciting sensory experience—and wear comfy shoes! Central Market (also known as Varvakios Agora) doesn’t need much of an introduction, and there’s no way you’ll miss it while exploring central Athens. Just follow your nose and it will lead you straight to the action (though as a fair warning, the bustling meat and fish halls carry a very strong aroma!). It’s a wonderful contrast after visiting [high-end boutiques](#4) nearby.

- **Must-Buy Items:** Greek oregano, Kalamata olives, fresh feta, and local honey
- **Pro Tip:** Visit early in the morning for the freshest produce and vibrant local energy

## 7. Enny Di Monaco

If you’re hunting for Christian Louboutin heels, annoyed because smaller shoe sizes disappear overnight on online luxury sites, and 50% off sounds like a dream deal, definitely visit [Enny Di Monaco](https://www.ennydimonaco.com/) in the upscale Kolonaki neighborhood. During seasonal sales in August and February, this high-end boutique offers incredible discounts. When I visited in August looking for wedding shoes, the shop assistants were exceptionally welcoming and helpful—not even a bit "sniffy." Louboutin heels were on sale at 50% off! While small sizes (35–37) were mostly what remained, if you fall into that shoe size range, you’re in luck because those sizes stay available longer here!

If you’ve read my previous Athens-related posts, you know shopping in Athens is just one of many wonderful experiences this city has to offer. Whether you’re a frugal fashionista hunting for outlet deals or a luxury enthusiast seeking designer fashion, I’m sure you’ll enjoy every minute of your trip. Since Athens is constantly evolving with new shops and hidden boutiques, I’ll keep exploring and update this guide with new favorite spots whenever I return. Happy shopping!
