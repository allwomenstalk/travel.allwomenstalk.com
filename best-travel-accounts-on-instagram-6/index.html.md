# 21 Best Travel Accounts on Instagram to Follow for Constant Inspiration

Hey there world traveler! If seeing the world is on your bucket list, you'll love these inspirational **travel accounts on Instagram**. But first, here's your handy **travel tip of the day**: carry digital and physical copies of your credit cards and passport, stored separately, just in case of an emergency while on the road.

Whether you're looking for budget travel hacks, solo adventure guides, or breathtaking landscape photography, following the right Instagram creators can spark your next big journey. Here are 21 sensational Instagram travel accounts to fill your feed with daily inspiration.

**Key Travel Categories Covered in This Guide:**

- **Budget Travel & Flight Hacks:** Smart strategies for affordable trips.

- **Solo Female Travel Guides:** Inspiring tips for independent explorers.

- **Landscape & Adventure Photography:** High-definition imagery from remote destinations.

## 1. @triphackr

Created by Clint Johnston, [Triphackr](https://triphackr.com) focuses on helping travelers uncover budget hacks, maximize credit card rewards, and travel smarter around the world. If you want to stretch your budget while exploring off-the-beaten-path destinations, as discussed in [solo female travel guides](#11), this account is an essential resource for practical advice.

## 2. @prettylittlelondon

Curated by Andrea and Sara, [Pretty Little London](https://prettylittlelondon.com) captures the charming streets, cozy cafes, and architectural beauty of London. From secret alleyways to festive seasonal decor, their feed provides constant inspiration for UK travelers and city break enthusiasts alike.

## 3. @jordhammond

Jord Hammond is renowned for his vibrant color palettes and striking compositions of Asian landscapes and cultural landmarks. His photography transforms everyday travel scenes into dreamlike visual stories that evoke instant wanderlust.

## 4. @lebackpacker

Johan Lolos, known as @lebackpacker, is an outdoor adventure photographer whose imagery showcases rugged mountains, serene lakes, and untouched wilderness. His shots inspire outdoor lovers to lace up their boots and head into nature.

## 5. @brendanvanson

Brendan van Son is a travel photographer and creator who documents his worldwide journeys with complete authenticity. Alongside stunning landscape visuals, he shares behind-the-scenes travel tips and gear advice for aspiring photographers.

## 6. @kirstenalana

Kirsten Alana combines high-end luxury photography with deep storytelling. Her captions offer thoughtful insights into historical sites, cultural conservation, and sustainable travel practices, making her feed both beautiful and informative.

## 7. @passionpassport

Passion Passport is a global community of travelers, storytellers, and photographers. Their curated feed highlights unique perspective shots from creators all over the globe, encouraging followers to discover new cultures and unexpected destinations.

## 8. @danielkordan

Daniel Kordan’s gallery features breathtaking landscapes from Greenland to Antarctica. His mastery of lighting and natural elements makes every photo feel like a piece of high art from the far corners of the Earth.

## 9. @chrisburkard

Renowned adventure photographer [Chris Burkard](https://www.chrisburkard.com) captures cold-water surf sessions, icy fjords, and dramatic landscapes. For broader outdoor inspiration, pair Chris's feed with [Daniel Kordan's Arctic captures](#8).

## 10. @chelseakauai

Chelsea Yamase (@chelseakauai) blends free-diving, mountain hiking, and mindful living into her travel content. Her vibrant videos and encouraging posts inspire travelers to stay active and stay connected with the environment.

## 11. @bemytravelmuse

Kristin Addis runs [Be My Travel Muse](https://www.bemytravelmuse.com), an essential hub for solo female adventurers. She provides practical safety tips, detailed itineraries, and encouraging advice for women exploring the world independently.

## 12. @landlopers

Matt Long of LandLopers shares experiential travel reviews, culinary highlights, and luxury travel guides. His approachable tone helps travelers discover memorable experiences without unnecessary stress.

## 13. @jeremyjauncey

As the founder of Beautiful Destinations, Jeremy Jauncey shares high-impact travel photography alongside updates on sustainable tourism initiatives worldwide.

## 14. @laurenepbath

Known as Australia's first professional Instagrammer, Lauren Bath focuses on wildlife, landscape photography, and regional tourism, presenting rich visual stories of the Australian continent.

## 15. @beautifuldestinations

With millions of followers, Beautiful Destinations showcases short video clips and stunning photos from top travel creators. Similar to community hubs like [@passionpassport](#7), it is a premier source for global travel motivation.

## 16. @leeabbamonte

Lee Abbamonte holds the distinction of being the youngest American to visit every country in the world. His feed features straightforward travel reviews, sports travel commentary, and globetrotting adventures.

## 17. @everythingeverywhere

Gary Arndt has been traveling non-stop since 2007, documenting UNESCO World Heritage sites and historic landmarks across all seven continents with impressive detail.

## 18. @traveldaveuk

Dave Brett of Travel Dave UK focuses on budget solo travel, offering practical advice on hostel stays, cheap flights, and navigating new cities on a budget.

## 19. @thenomadsoasis

The Nomads Oasis curates peaceful, tranquil travel aesthetics featuring cozy escapes, serene beaches, and lush tropical hideaways designed to help you unwind.

## 20. @polabur

Polina Burashnikova (@polabur) shares chic European lifestyle photography, colorful cityscapes, and stylish coffee shop recommendations from Amsterdam and beyond.

## 21. @wonderful_places

Wonderful Places is one of Instagram's largest travel curation accounts, featuring fairy-tale castles, hidden beaches, and striking natural wonders from creators around the world.
