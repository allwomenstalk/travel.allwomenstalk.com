# 7 of the Most Exciting Amusement Parks in the World to Visit

The attendance in exciting amusement parks around the world is on the rise, and it serves as no surprise! The thrill of the rollercoasters, the screams of excitement, and the adrenaline highs are all part of a fun-filled experience in the most family-friendly places. Especially when school is out, there is no better way to celebrate summer than with a trip to an amusement park. If you've ever wondered what is the most visited amusement park in the world or searched for the most beautiful amusement park in the world, you know that finding the perfect destination requires a bit of planning. So if you are an amusement park junkie, here are the most exciting amusement parks in the world you have yet to visit!

Before heading out for a day of high-speed coasters and non-stop entertainment, keeping a few trip essentials in mind will ensure you have a seamless day:

- Wear comfortable walking shoes with proper arch support for long walking distances.

- Plan smart hairstyles for amusement parks—such as double braids or secure buns—to keep hair tangle-free on fast rides.

- Stay hydrated by carrying a refillable water bottle and pack SPF 50+ sunscreen.

- Download official park mobile apps in advance to check live ride wait times and show schedules.

## 1. Magic Kingdom, Florida

![Magic Kingdom, Florida](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/eu/pd/j1fyb3q3_600x397.jpg)

Disney is really dominating the list, owning some of the most visited spots all over the world, including the Magic Kingdom in Florida. As the first built of the four theme parks at Walt Disney World Resort, it stands as one of the most exciting amusement parks in the world. It is known for its classic attractions, enchanting fireworks, and musical parades featuring beloved Disney characters. For complete details on park hours, ticket options, and special events, check out the [official Walt Disney World website](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/). If you enjoy this iconic location, you will also love comparing its magic to its sister park, [Disneyland, California](#7).

## 2. Everland, South Korea

![Everland, South Korea](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/e5/fn/mm378ucv_600x399.jpg)

Everland is one of South Korea’s largest amusement parks, usually marketed for families and couples alike. Not only is it known for its thrilling rides and attractions—such as the world-famous T-Express wooden coaster—but it is also famous for its breathtaking gardens and seasonal flower displays. Each season, Everland transforms with vibrant themes throughout the year, including the Rose Festival, Summer Splash, Happy Halloween, and Winter Wonderland. You can discover seasonal event schedules and visitor passes on the [official Everland site](https://www.everland.com/).

## 3. Lego Land, California

![Lego Land, California](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/u3/rx/f381eheo_600x400.jpg)

Lego Land is an incredible kid-focused theme park located in Carlsbad, California, bringing the world-famous Danish brick concept to life. With as many as fifty different interactive rides—such as Aquazone Wave Racers, Technic Coaster, and Driving School—it’s a fantastic place to take younger children for age-appropriate fun without standing in ridiculously long lines. The park is thoughtfully divided into distinct areas featuring roller coasters, water rides, and an ocean aquarium. When prepping for a day here, choosing functional hairstyles for theme parks like braided pigtails helps keep kids comfortable on every ride. If you're building a full coaster itinerary, consider combining this visit with a stop at [Disneyland, California](#7) or heading east toward [Cedar Point, Ohio](#5).

## 4. Ocean Park, Hong Kong

![Ocean Park, Hong Kong](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/lc/ml/fp5onv3x_600x400.jpg)

Ocean Park is a marine mammal park, oceanarium, animal theme park, and exciting amusement destination all in one! What makes this location so impressive is how seamlessly it blends education, marine conservation, and high-energy entertainment into a one-of-a-kind experience. You’ll never come closer to ocean life than here, with spectacular panda exhibits and cliffside cable car rides. Plus, Hong Kong residents enjoy free admission on their birthdays!

## 5. Cedar Point, Ohio

![Cedar Point, Ohio](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/j8/ah/lxhsap2m_600x391.jpg)

Cedar Point is the second oldest operating amusement park in the United States and remains one of the ultimate thrill destinations on the planet. Renowned as the "Roller Coaster Capital of the World," Cedar Point offers endless entertainment in the form of record-breaking rides, live stage shows, and vibrant nightlife along Lake Erie. With fresh additions and upgraded attractions added regularly, you can always count on having an unforgettable adventure. To explore coaster stats and trip planning guides, visit the [official Cedar Point website](https://www.cedarpoint.com/). Make sure to test out sturdy hairstyles for roller coasters, like secure buns or high ponytails, before tackling extreme drops!

## 6. Efteling, Netherlands

![Efteling, Netherlands](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/tk/vt/tmq7qod7_600x375.jpg)

Efteling in the Netherlands is praised by travelers around the globe as a magical haven for family fun at an affordable price. Visitors are consistently captivated by the park's unique fairytale atmosphere and immersive storytelling. From adrenaline-pumping coasters for adults to gentle dark rides for young children, Efteling caters to every age group. Delicious dining options and enchanting evening entertainment ensure your entire day remains filled with wonder.

## 7. Disneyland, California

![Disneyland, California](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/bj/a9/mbzfiv2y_600x375.jpg)

Oh Disneyland, the most magical and happiest place on Earth! It is a destination everyone should experience at least once in their lifetime to witness the magic firsthand and create lifelong memories. From timeless dark rides and thrilling modern attractions to nighttime fireworks and character greetings, everything the park offers is worth every penny. Whether you have already explored [Magic Kingdom, Florida](#1) or are embarking on your first Disney trip, this iconic park never ceases to amaze.

Make sure to visit some of these world-famous amusement parks for quality entertainment you won’t find anywhere else! When preparing for your trip, don't forget to pair your outfit with cute hairstyles for roller coasters so you stay picture-ready after every drop.
