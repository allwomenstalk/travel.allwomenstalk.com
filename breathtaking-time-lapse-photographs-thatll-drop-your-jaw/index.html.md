# 17 Breathtaking Time\-Lapse Photographs That'll Blow Your Mind

We've all seen time-lapse photos and wondered at the movement and intricacy of them, but Los Angeles-based photographer Daniel Marker-Moore has taken the concept one step further, grafting his time-lapse photos into one jaw-dropping image. By slicing multiple still frames captured across hours from day to night and blending them into a single composition, he creates a mesmerizing visual timeline of cityscapes and natural landscapes. Here's a look at his breathtaking "time-slice" series... prepare to have your mind blown!

## 1. New York

![New York](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/qw/at/xfsjgxb256902b5f41f98512163874_600x401.jpg)

The iconic Manhattan skyline is transformed as daylight slowly yields to sparkling city lights. Each vertical band showcases a different moment in the transition from golden hour sunset to deep dusk.

## 2. Shanghai

![Shanghai](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/rl/il/r2os85d956902b5f7d5af043489633_600x400.jpg)

Shanghai's futuristic skyline glows dramatically in this time-slice photograph. The contrast between bright afternoon skies and vibrant neon night lights illuminates the city's incredible architectural energy.

## 3. Hollywood

![Hollywood](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/hl/yr/ggv3b2i056902b6002139698644997_600x400.jpg)

Overlooking Hollywood, this photo captures the changing colors of the California sky above the bustling hills. Below is a glance at key elements of Marker-Moore's time-slice process:

- Exposure blending: Combining dozens of individual photos into diagonal or vertical slices.
- Time duration: Capturing sunset-to-twilight over a 2 to 4-hour shooting session.
- Precision alignment: Keeping the camera locked on a sturdy tripod for perfect continuity.

## 4. Toronto

![Toronto](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/v3/zw/peophg0r56902b5fcd45a829701740_600x400.jpg)
