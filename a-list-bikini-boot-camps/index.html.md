# What People Misunderstand About Celebrity Bikini Boot Camps

If you can afford to really splash out, you might want to join the A-listers at some of the ritzy bikini boot camps around the world. Exclusive spa retreats, swanky resorts, and top-tier service delivery are what’s on offer, together with the motivation to get your body feeling energized and swimsuit ready. The health retreat concept continually grows in popularity, so let’s take a peek into the world of celebrity fitness with these standout A-list bikini boot camps and wellness destinations.

| Destination | Location | Key Highlight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| SHA Wellness Clinic | Spain | Sierra Helada views & macrobiotic nutrition |
| Amansala Resort | Mexico | Tulum beachfront Bikini Bootcamp & yoga |
| Stoke Park | England | 350-acre stately home & championship sports |
| Gwinganna Retreat | Australia | Holistic Gold Coast rainforest sanctuary |
| The Ashram | USA | Santa Monica mountain hikes & detox |
| Chiva-Som | Thailand | World-class Thai hospitality & custom wellness |
| Cal-a-Vie | USA | French Provencal style retreat in California |

## 1. SHA Wellness Clinic, Spain

![SHA Wellness Clinic, Spain](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2013/05/1_sha-wellness-clinic-spain_600x400.jpg)

Occupying a glorious setting on the fringes of the Sierra Helada Natural Park, with stunning views of mountains and the Mediterranean, SHA is considered to be among the best wellness clinics in Europe. For its premier bikini boot camp programs, it offers a blend of natural therapies and nutrition plans delivered with modern Western techniques and deep Eastern wisdom. The program menu is full with rejuvenation, detox, weight management, and anti-stress plans. If you are looking for a similar getaway that focuses heavily on nature, check out this [holistic rainforest retreat](#4) in Australia.

[shawellnessclinic.com](https://shawellnessclinic.com/en/)

Famous fans of SHA include Simon Cowell, Madonna, Kylie Minogue, and Naomi Campbell.

## 2. Amansala Resort, Mexico

![Amansala Resort, Mexico](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2013/05/2_amansala-resort-mexico_600x349.jpg)

With all the effort of getting your body into tip-top shape, one of the best perks of bikini boot camps should most definitely be that the location is a stunner. Amansala can certainly lay claim to this. Located in Tulum on Mexico’s glorious Caribbean coast, Amansala features three distinct wellness programs: Bikini Bootcamp, Destination Detox, and Beach ‘n Bliss Yoga Retreat. With an eco-conscious philosophy, you’ll be enclosed in a charm of organic loveliness while putting your body through its paces. For guests interested in balancing fitness with tailored nutrition, SHA Wellness Clinic’s [natural therapy program](#1) is another excellent option.

[amansalaresort.com](https://www.amansala.com/)

Famous fans of Amansala include Charlize Theron, Cindy Crawford, Drew Barrymore, and Jude Law.

## 3. Stoke Park, England

![Stoke Park, England](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2013/05/3_stoke-park-england_600x400.jpg)

England may not be able to offer bikini boot camps in a tropical paradise, but it does the next best thing: a huge, sprawling, totally gorgeous stately home converted to a luxury hotel and leisure complex set in 350 acres of stunning English countryside. Everything at Stoke Park is top notch, featuring a state-of-the-art gym, health and spa suite, indoor pool, championship-standard golf course, tennis courts, and an award-winning restaurant. Tennis enthusiasts should note that the hotel hosts "The Boodles" charity event every year, attracting world-class athletes to its grass courts. While Stoke Park provides refined European luxury, those who prefer intensive mountain conditioning might favor [California’s secluded fitness retreat](#5).

[stokepark.com](https://www.stokepark.com/)

Famous fans of Stoke Park include Renee Zellweger, Hugh Grant, and Claudia Schiffer.

## 4. Gwinganna Lifestyle Retreat, Australia

![Gwinganna Lifestyle Retreat, Australia](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2013/05/4_gwinganna-lifestyle-retreat-australia_600x338.jpg)

Sited next to a tropical rainforest on Australia’s magnificent Gold Coast, Gwinganna is a place that truly rejuvenates the soul and uplifts the spirit—organically, of course. The approach is holistic and soulful with a nod to ancient Indigenous practices, such as the Spirit of Sound treatment where a soothing rhythm is played while you are massaged with basalt stones and rain sticks. Organic food is grown directly on the property, and you might find yourself sharing space with wallabies and being serenaded by native rainforest birds.

[gwinganna.com](https://www.gwinganna.com/)

Famous fans of Gwinganna include Hugh Jackman (who loved it so much he actually bought an ownership stake!) and Nicole Kidman.

Key features to look for in a top-tier bikini camp or health retreat:

- Tailored fitness and nutrition plans designed by certified experts
- Peaceful natural surroundings that promote physical and mental recovery
- Comprehensive wellness amenities including hydrotherapy, massages, and spa treatments

## 5. The Ashram, USA

![The Ashram, USA](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2013/05/5_the-ashram-usa_600x450.jpg)

With no outward sign of the lavish pampering you might expect from bikini boot camps favored by stars, The Ashram is tucked away in the foothills of the Santa Monica Mountains in California. Here the emphasis is on genuine transformation rather than pampering, and guests are expected to work hard to achieve real results. It is a focused digital detox environment with no public Internet, phones, or TVs—replaced instead by strenuous morning hikes through scenic mountain trails, dynamic daily gym classes, and fresh raw food meals.

[theashram.com](https://theashram.com/)

Famous fans of The Ashram include Oprah Winfrey, Gwyneth Paltrow, Catherine Zeta-Jones, and Colin Farrell.

## 6. Chiva-Som, Thailand

![Chiva-Som, Thailand](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2013/05/6_chiva-som-thailand_600x338.jpg)

One of the most exotic spa retreats in the world, Chiva-Som combines warm Thai hospitality with total luxury in a breathtaking setting in Hua Hin on the Gulf of Thailand. Chiva-Som helps you reach your wellness goals through a holistic approach where every guest receives an individual consultation and custom program. It offers the feel of a premier beachfront resort paired with the high-caliber guidance of an elite bikini boot camp and wellness sanctuary. If you enjoy customized beachfront training like Chiva-Som, you might also like [Mexico's Caribbean fitness getaway](#2).

[chivasom.com](https://www.chivasom.com/)

Famous fans of Chiva-Som include The Beckhams, Kate Moss, and Elle Macpherson.

## 7. Cal-a-Vie, USA

![Cal-a-Vie, USA](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/travel/2013/05/7_cal-a-vie-usa_600x399.jpg)

California has more than its fair share of bikini boot camps and spa retreats, but a long-standing favorite for celebrities is Cal-a-Vie in Vista, located just north of San Diego. Hosting only a limited number of guests at a time, Cal-a-Vie guarantees highly personalized attention to help you align your body, mind, and spirit. Rolling hills frame this Mediterranean-inspired sanctuary (complete with a signature French windmill), where guest villas are decorated with authentic French Provençal antiques. While the workout schedule is rigorous, the results are undeniable.

[cal-a-vie.com](https://www.cal-a-vie.com/)

Famous fans of Cal-a-Vie include Julia Roberts, Ricki Lake, Russell Crowe, and Uma Thurman.
